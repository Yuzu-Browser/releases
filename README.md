# Yuzu

A fast, native web browser for Mac, iPhone and iPad, in the spirit of [Arc](https://arc.net): a vertical sidebar,
Spaces, pinned tabs and favorites, and the same sidebar synced across your devices through iCloud.

Yuzu is written in Swift and SwiftUI on WebKit, with no Chromium and no Electron.

## Download

**[Download Yuzu for Mac](https://github.com/Yuzu-Browser/releases/releases/latest/download/Yuzu.dmg)**
(macOS 26 or later, notarized by Apple). Open the disk image and drag Yuzu to Applications.

Yuzu is free and needs no account. Signing in to iCloud on your Mac is only used to sync your Spaces, pinned tabs and
favorites with your other devices.

The iPhone and iPad app is in beta on TestFlight.

## Features

- A sidebar with Spaces, pinned tabs, favorites and folders, synced between Mac, iPhone and iPad
- A command bar to open addresses, search, and switch tabs
- Private windows, a built-in content blocker, downloads, find in page, Reader
- Web extensions (Manifest V2 and V3, from the Chrome Web Store or Firefox Add-ons)
- Can be set as your default browser

## Status

Yuzu is in early development: expect rough edges. This repository only hosts its releases.
